03 / Selected work

Four systems, built end to end.

Each one started with a messy dataset and a question worth answering. Here’s what came out.

In progress 01 / Flagship

ThinkAlpha

Extracting alpha from unstructured data

A hybrid RAG + agentic neural pipeline that pulls alpha signals out of unstructured financial data — SEC filings, earnings transcripts and news — and tests them the way a quant desk would.

  • Agentic retrieval over filings, transcripts & news
  • Point-in-time validation to keep look-ahead bias out
  • Signal scoring with IC, Sharpe, beta & confidence calibration
  • Agentic AI
  • RAG
  • Financial NLP
  • Backtesting
Schematic · point-in-timeOut-of-sample

Not live performance dataResearch build

02

Aurelius

Stock price movement prediction

Reads real-time Google News sentiment to forecast stock movement. The core was rebuilt on a Transformer architecture, replacing the original LSTM and adding convolutional layers for local pattern capture.

65–85%Average live signal confidence
  • NLP
  • Sentiment
  • Transformers
03

EstateVision

Property price predictor

A regression pipeline trained across 40 areas of Indore, forecasting property prices 5, 7 and 10 years out — then classifying each area into growth categories.

R² 0.94 · 90%+Regression / classification
  • Regression
  • Forecasting
  • Indore
04

CrediCoach

Credit risk predictor

A hybrid ensemble of Random Forest, XGBoost and a Keras neural network trained on 2,000+ credit records. Gemini turns each prediction into a plain-language risk breakdown.

92% accuracyOn 2,000+ training records
  • Ensemble
  • XGBoost
  • Gemini API
